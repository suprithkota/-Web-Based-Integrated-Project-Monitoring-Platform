The Challenge

Mega public infrastructure projects often experience unforeseen schedule delays, cost escalation, land acquisition hurdles, and contractor coordination bottlenecks that compound before administrative intervention occurs.

Existing Ecosystem (PAIMANA)

India's PAIMANA portal provides comprehensive project monitoring, expenditure tracking, milestones, and status reporting. It serves as the authoritative single source of truth for what is happening currently.

Our Innovation Layer

ProjectPulse AI complements PAIMANA by transforming backward-looking monitoring data into forward-looking predictive risk models, explainable root-cause attribution, automated early warnings, and scenario simulations.
